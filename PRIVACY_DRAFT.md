## Page copy (as it would appear at /privacy/)

# Privacy Policy

Salzenith operates salzenith.com. This page explains what information
is — and is not — collected when you visit this website or contact us,
and how enquiry information is handled.

## What this website does not do

- This website is a static information and enquiry site. It has no
  user accounts, no checkout and no payment processing.
- This website sets no cookies of its own and uses no browser storage
  to track you.
- No analytics are currently active on this website. If analytics are
  enabled in the future, this policy will be updated first to name the
  service and what it collects.

## Information you send us

- The quote form on this site does not submit anything to a server.
  It opens your own email app with your answers pre-filled, addressed
  to info@salzenith.com. We only receive what you choose to send from
  your email app, and sending it is your action.
- If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever
  you include — typically your name, company, country and enquiry
  details (products, quantities, specifications, destination port).
- We use enquiry information to respond to you, prepare quotations and
  samples, and — where an order follows — to arrange production,
  inspection, documentation and shipment.
- **[CONFIRM]** Enquiry and order details are shared only where needed
  to fulfil a request — for example with inspection agencies, freight
  and shipping partners, and documentation providers involved in your
  order.
- **[CONFIRM]** We do not sell personal information.
- **[CONFIRM — period needed]** Enquiry emails are kept for [period —
  e.g. as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting order,
  plus normal business record-keeping], then deleted or archived.

## Hosting

- This site is hosted with Hostinger. As with any web host, standard
  server logs (such as IP address, time of visit and pages requested)
  may be generated by the hosting infrastructure to operate and secure
  the site. We do not use hosting logs to identify or profile visitors.

## Your choices

- You can contact us at info@salzenith.com at any time to ask what
  enquiry information we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask for
  it to be deleted.
- Salzenith — Sakesar Warcha Road, Near Warcha Phatak, Quaidabad,
  District Khushab, Punjab 41330, Pakistan.
